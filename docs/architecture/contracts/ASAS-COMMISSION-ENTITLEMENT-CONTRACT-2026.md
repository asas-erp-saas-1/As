# ASAS Commission Entitlement Contract — 2026

**Status:** PROPOSED — implementation blocked
**Conference:** C02 → C06 boundary

## 1. Principle

`Commission Entitlement ≠ Lead Ownership ≠ Current Assignment ≠ Reservation Attribution`

Commission is a Finance-owned downstream commercial fact derived from authoritative sales facts and an approved commission rule/version.

## 2. Source facts

Commission calculation may consume, subject to the approved business model:

- reservation/sale identity;
- project and unit;
- commercial agency;
- responsible contributors;
- attribution snapshot;
- applicable agreement/relationship;
- commission rule/version;
- eligible base amount;
- status milestones;
- cancellation/refund/clawback facts.

No commission entitlement may be inferred solely from the current lead owner, current assignee, team membership, or project assignment.

## 3. Rule versioning

A commission calculation must identify the rule/version that produced it. Changing a future rule must not silently rewrite historical accrued entitlements.

## 4. Lifecycle

Conceptual lifecycle:

`Eligible → Calculated → Accrued → Approved → Paid`

with controlled correction/reversal/clawback paths. Exact statuses require Finance conference closure.

## 5. Separation from ledger

Commission entitlement is a commercial subledger/read-model concept until Finance establishes the authoritative accounting treatment. Once an amount becomes an authoritative posted financial fact, it follows immutable ledger/reversal rules.

## 6. Disputes

Disputed attribution or commission must use explicit dispute/correction workflows. Historical source facts and prior calculations remain auditable.

## 7. Cross-organization privacy

A Developer may receive the commission/performance information explicitly permitted by the commercial relationship. Agency-internal compensation arrangements remain private unless explicitly shared or contractually required.

## 8. Required closure inputs

Before implementation:

- commission rule taxonomy;
- relationship agreement model;
- eligible base definition;
- milestone/event mapping;
- cancellation/clawback semantics;
- currency/rounding policy;
- accounting treatment;
- authorization matrix;
- audit/event contract;
- reconciliation model.
