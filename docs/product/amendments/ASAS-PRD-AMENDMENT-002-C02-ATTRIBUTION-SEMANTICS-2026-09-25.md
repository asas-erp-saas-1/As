# ASAS PRD Amendment 002 — Lead Ownership / Assignment / Attribution

**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Status:** PROPOSED — FOUNDER REVIEW REQUIRED
**Parent:** `docs/product/ASAS-PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS-BASELINE-2026.md`

## Product requirement

ASAS must distinguish CRM ownership, operational assignment, acquisition source, commercial credit, and commission entitlement.

Users must be able to understand who currently owns/workloads a lead without confusing that with who generated the lead, who performed a visit, who receives sales credit, or who is financially entitled to commission.

## Developer experience

A Developer with authorized project access may see partner performance projections such as leads, visits, offers, reservations and sales, with attribution by Agency where policy permits.

Developer visibility must not expose another Agency's private CRM notes or unrelated private records.

## Agency experience

An Agency must retain its CRM ownership and private operational context while collaborating with Developers on authorized Projects. Team/project assignments control employee access.

## Auditability

Ownership transfers, assignments, attribution corrections and milestone attribution must be traceable.

## Status

Product semantics are proposed from the engineering conference. Founder/product approval remains required before this becomes product authority.
